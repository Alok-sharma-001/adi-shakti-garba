Good to know

Questions? Answers

How will I receive my ticket?

Once you pay, your e-ticket with a unique barcode appears on screen. Download it, or take a screenshot, and show it at the entry gate. Your tickets are also saved under "My Tickets" on this device.

Is traditional attire compulsory?

Traditional attire is strongly encouraged: chaniya choli, kediyu, kurta and similar outfits. It's a big part of the fun, and the best-dressed get prizes!

Will dandiya sticks be available at the venue?

Yes, dandiya sticks will be on sale at the venue. You are also welcome to bring your own.

Are children allowed? Do they need a ticket?

Absolutely, it's a family-friendly event! Children under 5 enter free with a ticketed adult. Children 5 and above need a pass.

Can I get a refund or change my night?

Tickets are non-refundable, but you can request a change of night up to 24 hours before the event by contacting the organisers on WhatsApp.

I paid but didn't get my ticket. What should I do?

Don't worry. Message Om Sharma or Deependra Singh on WhatsApp with your UTR / transaction ID and a payment screenshot, and they will issue your ticket manually.
